Key capabilities

What CSM machinery delivers for your tube fabrication workflow.

Complex Bend Geometry

Multi-radius, multi-plane bending in a single setup — CSM CNC benders handle tight CLR ratios, compound bends and complex tube routing without re-clamping or repositioning.

Offline Programming

3D bend simulation and offline programming software — create and verify bend programs on screen, detect collisions and optimise tooling before any tube is loaded on the machine.

Quality Assurance

Integrated bend measurement and SPC data collection — every bend is verified against tolerance, with full traceability and documentation for automotive and aerospace requirements.
