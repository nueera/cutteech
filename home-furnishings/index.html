Home Furnishings

Automation for home furnishing production.

Upholstery, curtains, and home textile manufacturing demand flexibility across materials and batch sizes. Cuttech delivers cutting, spreading, and workflow automation that keeps furnishing production agile and profitable.

Challenges in home furnishings production

Furnishing manufacturers juggle material diversity, order variability, and the constant pressure to reduce waste.

Material variety

Furnishing production runs span heavy upholstery fabrics, delicate sheers, technical textiles, leather, and foam. Each material demands different cutting parameters, blade types, and handling methods that manual processes struggle to accommodate efficiently.

Batch flexibility

From large contract orders to small custom runs, furnishing manufacturers must switch between high-volume and made-to-order production frequently. Changeover times between styles and materials eat into productive hours and increase unit costs.

Cutting accuracy & material yield

Expensive upholstery fabrics and leather represent a significant share of product cost. Suboptimal marker layouts and imprecise cuts waste material that directly erodes margins, especially on patterned and directional fabrics.

Solutions for home furnishing makers

Cutting, spreading, and workflow systems built for the material and batch diversity of furnishing production.

Automatic cutting machines

CNC knife cutting systems that handle the full range of furnishing materials—from heavy upholstery to delicate sheers. Vacuum hold-down and multi-tool heads enable clean, accurate cuts without manual recuts or material waste.

  • Multi-tool: oscillating, drag, punch
  • Conveyor and static table options
  • Patterned fabric matching support

Spreading systems

Automated fabric spreading machines that prepare accurate, tension-free layups for cutting. Programmable spread modes handle zig-zag, one-way, and face-to-face configurations with automatic edge alignment.

  • Cradle and roll loading options
  • Electronic tension control
  • Multi-lay spreading up to 22 cm

Workflow automation

Digital pattern management, automated marker making, and production scheduling that connects design to cutting seamlessly. Reduce manual handoffs, eliminate pattern errors, and maintain traceability from order to dispatch.

  • PLM integration & digital patterns
  • Automated nesting & costing
  • Production reporting dashboards
