About Cuttech

Bringing global manufacturing technology to Indian production floors.

What Cuttech does differently.

Not a catalog reseller. A technical team that maps your process, recommends the right machinery and stays through deployment.

Process-first approach

Every engagement starts with understanding your materials, throughput targets and quality requirements — not with a product pitch. The recommendation follows the workflow.
