Health Care

Precision cutting for healthcare applications.

Medical product manufacturing requires controlled environments, validated processes, and documented quality at every step. Cuttech delivers precision cutting and inspection systems that meet the stringent demands of healthcare production.

Challenges in healthcare manufacturing

Medical product production operates under regulatory scrutiny that demands precision, consistency, and complete traceability.

Technical textiles & advanced materials

Healthcare products use specialised materials—medical-grade nonwovens, laminates, films, and composites—each with unique cutting characteristics. Inconsistent cuts compromise seal integrity, barrier performance, and device function.

Controlled environment requirements

Manufacturing in cleanrooms and controlled environments limits equipment choices. Cutting systems must operate without generating particulates, must be easily sanitised, and must comply with environmental monitoring protocols.

Quality documentation & compliance

Every medical product must be produced under documented, validated processes. Maintaining complete quality records—from incoming material inspection through final cut verification—is resource-intensive and non-negotiable.

Solutions for healthcare production

Precision cutting, inspection, and automation systems purpose-built for medical manufacturing environments.

Precision cutting systems

CNC cutting machines with fine-tipped blades and laser options engineered for delicate medical materials. Consistent, burr-free cuts on nonwovens, films, and laminates maintain material integrity critical to product performance.

  • Ultra-fine blade & laser options
  • Low-particulate operation
  • Automated tool calibration

Quality control & inspection

LaserQC inspection systems that verify cut part dimensions against CAD nominal with ± 0.05 mm accuracy. Automated colour-coded reports provide immediate pass/fail determination and full traceability for quality records.

  • 100% first-article inspection
  • Automated deviation reporting
  • SPC data collection

Automated workflows

Digital process workflows that enforce production sequences, capture operator actions, and generate complete batch records. Eliminate paper-based documentation and reduce the risk of human error in medical manufacturing.

  • Enforced production sequences
  • Electronic batch records
  • Real-time process monitoring

Why Cuttech for healthcare

Controlled environment expertise, compliance focus, and validation support for medical manufacturing.

Controlled environment expertise

We understand the constraints of cleanroom and controlled-area manufacturing. Our cutting systems are selected and configured for low particulate generation, easy sanitisation, and compatibility with environmental monitoring requirements.

Compliance focus

Healthcare production demands adherence to ISO 13485, FDA 21 CFR Part 820, and other regulatory frameworks. Our solutions generate the documentation and audit trails necessary to demonstrate compliance during inspections and certifications.

Validation support

We assist with Installation Qualification (IQ), Operational Qualification (OQ), and Performance Qualification (PQ) protocols for cutting and inspection systems. Our validation documentation packages accelerate your qualification timelines.
