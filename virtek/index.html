Product line

Virtek's laser-based systems for inspection, projection and assembly guidance.

LaserQC

Fast, accurate laser inspection system that verifies flat parts against CAD data in seconds — eliminates manual checking and catches deviations before they reach assembly.

  • Inspect parts in under 10 seconds
  • Automatic deviation heat maps
  • First-article inspection reports

IRIS 3D

Laser projection system that projects 3D part outlines directly onto tooling and work surfaces — guides operators through complex layup, ply placement and assembly sequences.

  • 3D projection onto curved surfaces
  • Multi-projection workstation linking
  • Real-time projection accuracy

ActiveTrack

Real-time part tracking and verification system that monitors assembly progress, confirms part placement and validates process completion on the production floor.

  • Live assembly step verification
  • Automatic process documentation
  • Error-proofed workflows
