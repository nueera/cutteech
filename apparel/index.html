Apparel

Cutting room automation for apparel production.

From digital pattern creation to automated spreading and cutting, Cuttech delivers end-to-end cutting room solutions that reduce fabric waste and accelerate production speed.

Industry challenges

Apparel manufacturers operate on thin margins where every centimeter of fabric and every minute of labor counts.

Material Waste

Fabric accounts for 60–70% of garment cost. Inefficient manual cutting and poor marker layouts waste 5–15% of material — directly eroding already thin margins on every production run.

Pattern Efficiency

Manual pattern making is slow and inconsistent. Grading errors propagate across sizes, and the time from design to production-ready marker can stretch into weeks for complex styles.

Production Speed

Fast fashion demands shorter runs and faster turnarounds. Manual spreading, hand-cutting, and paper-based workflows simply can't keep up with the pace modern retail requires.
