Wind Energy

Projection and QC for wind energy manufacturing.

Wind turbine blade and component manufacturing demands precision at massive scale. Cuttech delivers laser projection, inspection, and tracking systems that ensure accuracy on large-format composite layups and assemblies.

Challenges in wind energy production

Manufacturing wind turbine components introduces scale, accuracy, and compliance challenges unlike any other industry.

Large-format inspection

Blade moulds, root inserts, and spar caps span metres in length. Traditional measurement tools cannot deliver the coverage or speed needed for 100% inspection of these oversized composite structures without bottlenecks.

Assembly accuracy at scale

Ply placement on curved blade surfaces requires exact positioning. Manual layup leads to fibre misalignment, wrinkles, and gaps that compromise structural integrity and aerodynamic performance of the finished blade.

Documentation & traceability

Wind energy projects demand rigorous quality records for every component. Manually documenting layup sequences, inspection results, and deviations is time-consuming and prone to errors that can delay certification.

Solutions for wind energy manufacturing

Laser-guided systems built for the scale and precision demands of wind turbine production.

Virtek laser projection

Project virtual templates directly onto moulds and layup surfaces to guide operators through ply placement. FlashAlign technology enables instant re-alignment, reducing setup from hours to seconds on large-format tooling.

  • Multi-projector workstation sharing
  • FlashAlign instant positioning
  • Curved surface projection

LaserQC inspection

Capture over 500 data points per second for rapid, accurate inspection of 2D and formed parts. Colour-coded deviation reports make it easy to identify out-of-tolerance areas directly on the shop floor.

  • ± 0.05 mm 2D accuracy
  • First-article inspection
  • SPC & quality reporting

ActiveTrack & production reporting

Real-time production tracking that records every step of the layup process. Automated documentation eliminates manual logging, ensures traceability, and provides instant visibility into workflow completion status.

  • Step-by-step layup verification
  • Automated production logs
  • Historical data retrieval
