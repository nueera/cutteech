Machine types

Cutting solutions matched to your material, volume and production style.

High-Ply Cutting

Automatic high-ply cutting machines for multi-layer spreads — up to 80 mm compressed height. Ideal for large-volume apparel and home textile production with continuous cutting capability.

  • Vacuum-held cutting surfaces
  • Round blade and drag knife options
  • Up to 80 mm cutting height

Single-Ply Cutting

Precision single-ply cutting for technical textiles, composites, automotive interiors and made-to-order garments that demand detailed pattern accuracy.

  • Oscillating knife and ultrasonic tools
  • Notch and drill marking
  • Pattern-matching vision

Spreading Machines

Automatic fabric spreaders with tension-free spreading, face-to-face or one-way layup and programmable ply counts for consistent material preparation.

  • Cradle and roll loading
  • Automatic edge alignment
  • Programmable spread parameters

Workflow integration

Connected cutting rooms — from marker making to bundled output.

Marker Making & Nesting

Automatic and interactive marker-making software that maximises fabric yield while respecting grain, print matching and size ratio constraints.

Cut-Order Planning

Optimise lay lengths, ply counts and size combinations to balance material savings with production capacity — directly linked to your cutting schedule.

Bundle Tracking & Labeling

Automatic bundle ticket printing and barcode tracking from cut to sew — real-time visibility into work-in-progress and production throughput.

Production benefits
