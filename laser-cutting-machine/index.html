Laser technology

Fibre and CO₂ laser platforms configured for your material and thickness range.

Fibre Laser Cutting

High-power fibre laser machines from 1 kW to 30 kW for fast, clean cutting of mild steel, stainless steel, aluminium, copper and brass — up to 40 mm thickness.

  • Flying optics and pallet changers
  • Auto-focus cutting heads
  • Low operating cost per part

CO₂ Laser Cutting

Proven CO₂ laser platforms for specialised applications including thick mild steel, non-metals and materials that benefit from longer wavelength absorption.

  • Up to 6 kW RF-excited sources
  • Mixed-gas cutting capability
  • Wide material compatibility

Tube & Profile Laser

Dedicated laser tube cutting machines that handle round, square and rectangular tubes with cutting, drilling and notching in a single operation.

  • 3D rotary cutting heads
  • Automatic bundle loading
  • Bevel cutting capability

Application areas

Laser cutting solutions deployed across India's top manufacturing sectors.

Sheet Metal Fabrication

Flat-sheet laser cutting for enclosures, brackets, panels and structural components with high repeatability and minimal post-processing.

Automotive Components

High-speed blanking and profiling of body panels, chassis parts and structural reinforcements for tier-1 and tier-2 suppliers.

Precision Manufacturing

Tight-tolerance cutting for medical devices, electronics enclosures, aerospace brackets and other high-value precision components.

System integration

Connect laser cutting into your complete digital fabrication workflow.

Nesting & Programming

Advanced nesting software that maximises sheet yield and generates optimised CNC programs directly from 3D CAD models — no manual coding required.

Material Handling

Automated pallet changers, tower storage systems and sheet loaders that keep lasers running continuously with minimal operator intervention.

Quality & Inspection

Post-cut inspection with Virtek LaserQC, real-time process monitoring and SPC integration for zero-defect production strategies.
