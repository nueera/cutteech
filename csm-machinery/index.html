CSM capabilities

Proven tube bending technology for complex geometries and high-volume production.

CNC Tube Bending

Full-electric and hybrid CNC tube bending machines with multi-axis control for tight-radius, complex bends in tube and pipe from 6 mm to 220 mm OD.

  • Multi-stack tooling configurations
  • Real-time springback compensation
  • Right-left bending capability

End Forming & Processing

Integrated end-forming, flaring, expanding and crimping stations that combine with benders for complete tube processing in a single setup.

  • Rotary end forming
  • Multi-station processing heads
  • In-line quality checks

Automation & Robotics

Robotic loading, unloading and part transfer systems that keep CSM benders running lights-out with consistent throughput and minimal operator intervention.

  • 6-axis robotic part handling
  • Automatic bundle loading
  • Vision-guided pick-and-place

Application areas

CSM machinery solving real bending challenges across industries.

Automotive Exhaust & Fluid Lines

High-volume production of exhaust systems, brake lines, fuel pipes and coolant tubes with tight tolerance and repeatability requirements.

HVAC & Refrigeration

Precision-bent copper, aluminium and steel tubing for heat exchangers, condenser coils and refrigeration circuit assemblies.

Structural & Furniture

Complex structural tube frames, furniture tubing and handrail systems requiring clean bends with minimal wall thinning.
