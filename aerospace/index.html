Industry challenges

Aerospace manufacturing demands perfection at every step. Here are the critical hurdles your production floor faces.

Tight Tolerances

Aerospace components require tolerances as tight as ±0.002". Manual inspection is too slow and error-prone for production volumes, leading to bottlenecks and costly rework.

Traceability Requirements

Every part must be fully documented from raw material to final assembly. Regulatory compliance (AS9100, NADCAP) demands detailed inspection records and process verification.

Complex Geometries

Composite layup, multi-axis forming, and compound-curve surfaces make traditional templating and measurement impractical. Operators need guided assembly at every step.
