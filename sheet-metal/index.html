Sheet Metal

Laser QC, projection and fabrication solutions.

From first-article inspection to laser-guided assembly and optimized nesting — Cuttech covers the entire sheet metal fabrication workflow with integrated technology.

Industry challenges

Sheet metal fabrication shops face persistent bottlenecks that erode margins and delay shipments.

First-Article Inspection

Traditional CMM inspection for first articles can take hours per part. Production machines sit idle while you wait for quality sign-off, creating costly bottlenecks at the start of every run.

Nesting Efficiency

Poor nesting wastes expensive sheet material. Manual layout and suboptimal algorithms leave significant scrap percentages on the table — directly eating into your profitability on every job.

Assembly Guidance

Complex weldments and multi-part assemblies rely on physical templates and fixtures that wear out, get lost, or require storage. Operator errors in placement lead to rework and delays.

Cuttech solutions

An integrated suite — from laser inspection to projection-guided assembly and intelligent nesting software.

LaserQC Inspection

Replace hours of CMM time with seconds of laser scanning. Capture over 500 data points per second with ±0.002" accuracy. Color-coded pass/fail results let operators make immediate decisions.

  • First-article inspection in minutes
  • 2D reverse engineering to CAD
  • SPC and quality reporting built-in

Virtek Projection

Eliminate physical templates. Project virtual laser outlines onto work surfaces to guide part placement, weld locations, and fastener positions. FlashAlign alignment is 20X faster than traditional methods.

  • Replace fixtures and templates
  • Multi-tasking across stations
  • Works on flat and curved surfaces

Hexagon Radan

Complete sheet metal CAD/CAM suite — from design and quoting through nesting, cutting, and bending. True-shape nesting algorithms maximize material utilization and reduce scrap.

  • Intelligent true-shape nesting
  • Integrated quoting module
  • CAM for cutting and bending
