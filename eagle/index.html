Eagle

Eagle cutting and plotting systems.

The fastest laser cutting machines in the world — with cutting speeds up to 150 m/min, 6G dynamic acceleration and fiber laser power from 1 to 40 kW.

Machines built for speed.

From high-speed mass production to continuous material flow, Eagle machines redefine what's possible on a sheet metal shop floor.

01

iNspire Series

State-of-the-art machines with linear motors on all axes, composite body structure and eVa cutting head. Cutting speeds up to 150 m/min, positioning speed 340 m/min, acceleration up to 6G. Fiber laser powers from 1 to 40 kW.

02

FlowIN Series

Integrated system for continuous material flow, simultaneous cutting and sorting. Every part sorted as efficiently as the machine cuts — no skeleton, no combs, no shape restrictions. Available in sheet and coil versions.

03

CraneMaster Automation

Loading and unloading system that simultaneously loads new material and unloads cut parts. Highly efficient cycle times for medium to high volume production. Available with eTower storage for 24/7 unsupervised operation.

Key capabilities.

Unmatched Speed

Up to 150 m/min cutting speed and 340 m/min positioning speed with 6G dynamic acceleration. The fastest laser machines on the market for high-volume production.

24/7 Automation

CraneMaster with eTower storage enables fully unsupervised production. Automatic loading, unloading and storage — maximizing machine utilization around the clock.

Lowest Cost Per Part

Intelligent construction, reduced energy consumption and minimal maintenance deliver the lowest operating cost per part in the industry. Burr-free cuts eliminate secondary operations.

Applications across industries.

Eagle systems serve sheet metal fabricators, automotive suppliers, aerospace manufacturers and general engineering workshops.

01

Sheet Metal Fabrication

High-speed cutting of thin to medium gauge steel, stainless steel and aluminum. FlowIN eliminates skeleton handling and enables continuous sorting.

02

Automotive & Tier-1 Supply

Volume production of structural and body components with repeatable accuracy. Automation-ready for unattended shifts and just-in-time delivery.

03

Aerospace & Precision

High-accuracy cutting of aerospace-grade alloys with minimal heat-affected zone. iNspire delivers burr-free edges and tight tolerances.
