DigiFlash

Supera

High-performance automatic cutting machine for apparel and technical textiles — precision cutting with vacuum hold-down, multiple tool options and automatic sharpening.

  • Continuous cutting capability
  • Oscillating and drag knife tools
  • Automatic blade sharpening

Digital workflow

Connected systems from design concept to cut part.

Pattern to Marker

Seamless flow from pattern creation through grading to automatic marker making — design a style once and move it through size scaling and nesting without data loss or re-entry.

Marker to Machine

Direct communication from Audaces marker software to the cutting machine — send optimised cut plans with tool assignments and sequencing straight to the Supera cutting table.

Cut-Order to Delivery

Plan cut orders by style, size ratio and delivery date — optimised lay planning linked to spreading and cutting schedules for on-time production output.

Production benefits

Measurable improvements across your cutting room operations.

Higher Fabric Yield

Automatic marker making delivers 2–5% fabric savings over manual nesting — directly improving margins on every production run across your entire style catalogue.

Faster Style Turnaround

Consistent Cut Quality

Computer-controlled knife paths, vacuum hold-down and automatic sharpening on the Supera ensure every piece meets specification — no re-cuts, no quality escapes.
