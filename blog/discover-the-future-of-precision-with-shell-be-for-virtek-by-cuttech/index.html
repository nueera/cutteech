Discover the Future of Precision with Shell Be for Virtek by Cuttech

Next-generation laser projection and precision measurement technology that transforms quality assurance on the production floor.

November 12, 2024 Laser Projection Quality Assurance Virtek

Precision in manufacturing has always been measured in fractions — fractions of a millimetre, fractions of a degree, fractions of a second. But as production complexity increases and tolerances tighten, the tools for ensuring precision must evolve beyond traditional measurement methods. Shell Be for Virtek by Cuttech represents this evolution: a system that doesn't just measure precision after the fact but projects it directly into the workflow, guiding operators in real time and verifying results instantly.

Laser projection meets real production

Virtek laser projection systems have been trusted by aerospace manufacturers, composite fabricators and precision assembly teams worldwide for over two decades. The technology works by projecting 3D CAD data directly onto tooling surfaces, showing operators exactly where to place components, apply material or perform operations. This eliminates the need for physical templates, reduces setup time and virtually eliminates alignment errors.

Shell Be for Virtek extends this capability with enhanced measurement and verification functions. The system can not only project the correct placement but also verify that the placement was executed correctly — closing the loop between instruction and verification in a single, integrated workflow. For manufacturers working to aerospace standards, automotive PPAP requirements or medical device compliance, this integration of projection and verification represents a significant step forward in process control.

The future of precision isn't about measuring more carefully after the fact — it's about embedding accuracy into every step of the process. Shell Be for Virtek makes that possible on the production floor, not just in the quality lab.

From aerospace to everyday precision

While Virtek technology was pioneered in aerospace composite layup — where misaligned plies can compromise structural integrity — its applications have expanded dramatically. Today, Cuttech deploys Virtek systems across a range of Indian manufacturing sectors:

  • Aerospace: Composite ply layup verification, assembly alignment and inspection of complex structures against CAD models
  • Sheet metal: Weld fixture alignment, bending verification and first-article inspection without contact measurement
  • Wind energy: Composite blade layup guidance and quality verification for large-scale turbine components
  • Automotive: Interior trim alignment, adhesive bead placement verification and sub-assembly positioning

What makes Shell Be for Virtek particularly valuable in the Indian context is its accessibility. Cuttech provides not just the hardware but the complete implementation framework — from application assessment and system configuration to operator training and ongoing technical support. This ensures that the technology is adopted effectively and delivers measurable improvements in quality, throughput and rework reduction from the first day of deployment.

For manufacturers evaluating precision technology investments, the key metric is not the capability of the system in isolation but the improvement it delivers in your specific production environment. Cuttech's process-first approach means that every Virtek deployment begins with understanding your workflow, your quality challenges and your production targets — so the system is configured to solve your problems, not just demonstrate technology.
