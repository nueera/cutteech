Gartex Exhibition: Showcasing Manufacturing Innovation

Cuttech at Gartex — live technology demonstrations, industry conversations and a glimpse into the future of Indian manufacturing.

November 20, 2024 Exhibition Manufacturing Innovation

The Gartex Exhibition has established itself as one of India's most significant platforms for garment and textile manufacturing technology. For Cuttech, it represents a critical opportunity to demonstrate how advanced automation — from automatic cutting machines to precision laser projection systems — is transforming production floors across the country.

Technology on display

At the Cuttech booth, visitors experienced live demonstrations of manufacturing technology that is already changing how Indian factories operate. The highlight was the demonstration of automatic cutting systems that combine speed with material efficiency — showing how intelligent nesting algorithms can reduce fabric waste by 3-5% compared to manual marker making, a figure that translates directly into cost savings for high-volume production environments.

Beyond cutting, Cuttech showcased its broader portfolio of precision manufacturing solutions. Virtek laser projection systems demonstrated how alignment and layup processes can be accelerated while improving accuracy. For aerospace and composite layup applications, the ability to project templates directly onto tooling eliminates the need for physical templates and reduces setup time from hours to minutes.

The conversations at Gartex confirmed what we see across the industry — manufacturers are no longer asking whether to automate, but where to start and how to implement effectively. That's exactly the gap Cuttech fills.

Conversations that matter

Exhibitions are ultimately about people and conversations. The Cuttech team spent three days discussing specific production challenges with factory owners, production managers and technical directors from across India's textile and garment manufacturing belt. These conversations revealed several consistent themes:

  • A growing demand for technology that integrates into existing workflows rather than requiring wholesale process redesign
  • Interest in modular automation that can scale with business growth, starting with a single process and expanding from there
  • Recognition that implementation support — training, process optimisation and after-sales service — is as important as the technology itself
  • A shift from cost-focused evaluation to value-focused evaluation, where ROI includes quality improvement and throughput gains alongside labour savings

These insights directly shape how Cuttech structures its technology partnerships. The process-first approach — understanding a client's specific workflow before recommending a solution — emerged from exactly this type of ground-level industry engagement. Gartex reinforced that this approach is not just preferred but expected by India's increasingly sophisticated manufacturing community.

For manufacturers who missed the exhibition or want to continue the conversation, Cuttech offers personalised technology assessments and on-site demonstrations. The exhibition may last three days, but the technology partnerships formed there are designed to deliver value for years.
